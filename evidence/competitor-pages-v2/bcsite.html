Description

Our most popular anti-skateboard device is also one of the most effective ways to stop rogue skateboarders. The Flat Radius Anti-Skateboard Guards are designed for walls with a 1/8” radius edge or a 90 degree sharp edge. They are perfect for protecting surface edges such as concrete retaining walls, planters, and pony walls however they can also be used with other materials such as: granite, marble, stone, block, brick, and other surfaces with a hard corner or edge.

These aluminum guards feature an anodized finish that looks great with any architecture style and are the best deterrent to stop skateboarders from ruining your property by grinding along walls and edges. Each guard is secured using security screws to prevent unauthorized tampering or removal.

Used by Contractors, Landscapers, Property Managers, Architects to deter unwanted skateboarding around their buildings premises, Flat Radius Anti-Skateboard Guards are the most common and fit the widest range of exterior furnishings, walls, fountains, and concrete planters of all the guards available. Skateboard prevention devices have been proven to reduce property damage, discourage loitering, and deter skaters.

This product is not intended for use on steps, stairs, sidewalk curb, or any pedestrian trafficked surfaces.

Specifications:

Dimensions: 2″ wide x 4 ¾” deep x 1″ tall
Material: 6000 Series Aluminum (6061-T6 Aluminum)
Drawing: Flat Radius Anti-Skateboard Guard Layout PDF

Kit Contains:

  • Anti-skateboard guards (20, 60, or 100 piece kits available)
  • Security screws for mounting the guards
  • Security screw bit for use with multi-drivers or drills
  • Concrete drill bit, epoxy.
  • Note: Purchasing per single unit only includes guards and security screws

Installation:

For most installations, the best placement is to put one guard at each end of your concrete wall approximately 12 to 18 inches from the end, and then install guards every 24 inches between the two ends. Do not install at grout joints.
