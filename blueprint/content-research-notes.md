## 1. Niche standards and certifications (CSA, AODA, OBC, CNB)

- **AODA / LAPHO Section 4.5.1** — handrail tactile-feature guidance. Continuous graspable surface in 38-50 mm rail diameter zone is mandatory along rail length. Skate stoppers must install **outside** the graspable zone (above or below) at 600-900 mm spacing.
- **OBC 3.4.6.5 / CNB 9.8.7.4** — handrail load capacity and graspable continuity for stairs and ramps in commercial buildings. Same continuity rule as AODA.
- **CSA Z614** — playground equipment standard, applies to bench seating in playgrounds. Specifies max protrusion height for fixed elements above seating surface.
- **NFPA 101 Life Safety Code** — emergency egress route requirements. Skate stoppers on stair handrails must not interfere with emergency descent.
- **Building Code of Quebec (CBC)** — provincial implementation of CNB; all installs in Quebec municipalities reference CBC compliance.
- **Heritage Buildings Policy (Federal)** — installations on federally heritage-designated property require Parks Canada approval before install. Reversibility analysis required.

## 5. Material engineering facts

- **304 stainless steel**: chromium 18-20% / nickel 8-10.5%. Pits in chloride environments after 5-7 years. Suitable inland Canada.
- **316 stainless steel**: chromium 16-18% / nickel 10-14% / molybdenum 2-3%. Resists chloride pitting. Required for coastal Canada.
- **316L (low-carbon)**: same as 316 but ≤0.03% carbon. Resists weld-zone sensitization. Preferred for transit deployments where field welding occurs.
- **Bronze patina (silicon bronze C65500 or similar)**: develops protective oxide layer, no maintenance, 30+ year life on heritage stone. Required for federal heritage submissions.
- **Powder-coat finish over 304 stainless**: brand-color matching for corporate deployments. UV-stable under 5-year warranty.
- **2-part epoxy (e.g., Hilti HIT-RE 500 V3, Simpson SET-XP)**: 70+ MPa compressive strength. Cures 24h at +5°C. Cold-weather variants cure to -10°C.
